What we do

Attar, made the slow way

Attar is fragrance as oil rather than spray — no alcohol, no aerosol, no filler. It sits closer to the skin, blooms more slowly, and lasts far longer than an eau de parfum because there is nothing volatile to evaporate off it.

Ours are blended and poured by hand at our Kamla Nagar counter, in small enough batches that we can smell every one before it goes into a bottle. Two sizes: a 6ml roll-on to try, a 12ml roll-on for when you already know.

Why non-alcoholic

No alcohol, on purpose

Alcohol is a carrier — it makes scent sprayable and cheap to bottle, then it takes the top notes with it as it evaporates. Oil does not. That is the whole reason an attar lasts a day on skin and weeks on cloth.

It also makes them suitable for anyone avoiding alcohol-based fragrance, and gentler on skin that reacts to sprays.
