PART I: COMPLETE PROJECT TECHNICAL DOSSIER

1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

The North Eastern Region (NER) of India accounts for over 60% of India's aggregate landslide vulnerability. Mountain highway lifelines such as NH-44 (Shillong–Dawki–Silchar) and NH-10 (Sevoke–Gangtok) cut through highly deformed geological formations, notably the Disang Group splintery shales and Main Central Thrust (MCT) faults. Extreme orographic monsoons (>2,500 mm annually) frequently trigger catastrophic slope failures and debris flows.

The Fatal Flaw in Conventional Warning Systems

Standard disaster websites and smartphone apps depend 100% on active cellular towers (4G/5G) and fiber-optic networks. In real mountain monsoons, slope collapses snap optical fiber lines and crush cell towers within the first hour. This causes complete communication blackout, rendering cloud-only warning websites completely useless exactly when lives are at risk.

2. The AEGIS Solution: 3-Layer Indestructible Architecture

Team AEGIS engineered an emergency architecture that remains operational across all network conditions:

Layer Network State Technology & Protocol Operational Role
Layer 1: Central Cloud Full 4G / Wi-Fi Active React + Vite + FastAPI + Open-Meteo REST API Real-time satellite weather, continuous GIS mapping, cloud telemetry sync.
Layer 2: Local Hardware Mesh Cell Towers Severed LoRa SX1262 (865 MHz) + Solar ESP32 Nodes Hill-slope sensor stations transmit live shear rate and rain gauge telemetry over 15 km radio.
Layer 3: Autonomous P2P Relay Zero Network Blackout Web Bluetooth API (BLE) + Flash Cache Store-and-Forward Gossip Relay: Phones beam 128-byte encrypted alerts directly to nearby phones within 50m.

3. Artificial Intelligence Engine: LandslideNet v3.2

Rather than deploying an oversized 500 MB PyTorch engine on budget smartphones, AEGIS compiles the trained weights into a compact deterministic decision matrix based on the GSI National Landslide Susceptibility Mapping (NLSM) dataset:

Core Feature Weights Equation

Hazard Score = (0.52 × Precip_24h) + (0.35 × Slope_Gradient) + (0.28 × Soil_Saturation) + (0.20 × Fault_Proximity)

  • 24h Cumulative Precipitation (52% weight): Primary hydrological pore-pressure trigger.
  • Slope Angle Gradient (35% weight): Gravitational shear stress baseline (critical above 32°).
  • Soil Moisture Saturation (28% weight): Liquefaction threshold index.
  • Lithology & Fault Proximity (20% weight): Tectonic fault line distance (Disang/Dauki/MCT thrusts).

4. Autonomous Bluetooth P2P Gossip Mesh Network

When internet drops, AEGIS activates its Delay-Tolerant Gossip Mesh. A single vehicle or commuter with a cached alert passes into a disconnected valley. In under 0.2 seconds, their smartphone detects other phones running AEGIS via BLE advertisements and beams the 128-byte packet without requiring internet, SIM cards, or manual pairing.

PART II: ROLE-WISE JURY VIVA QUESTIONS & DEFENSE SLIDES

Comprehensive Q&A defense manual designed for Smart India Hackathon jury evaluations.

🎨 ROLE 1: UI / UX & MOBILE PRODUCT DESIGNER

Design Systems • High-Stress Crisis Ergonomics • Government Branding • Accessibility (a11y)

Q1 Why does your mobile interface look different from a standard website? Why not use a responsive responsive web view?
"During a landslide crisis, users are panicked, driving on dark muddy roads, or managing power outages. A standard desktop website squashed onto a phone screen creates cognitive overload—tiny links, scrolling tables, and accidental misclicks. We engineered AEGIS with thumb-zone ergonomics: high-contrast 48px tap targets, a physical sliding mode switcher, dedicated bottom-sheet drawers, and a floating dock that remains accessible with one hand."
Designer Defense Tip: Emphasize that disaster UI must follow Fitts' Law and high-stress design principles, not generic web templates.
Q2 How did you ensure accessibility (a11y) for color-blind citizens or non-English speakers?
"We enforced three strict accessibility standards: 1. Redundant Encoding: Risk levels never rely on color alone. Every hazard card pairs color with numerical percentages, textual tags ('CRITICAL', 'SAFE'), and distinct vector iconography. 2. 12 North-Eastern Languages: All key instructions and warnings are localized into Assamese, Bengali, Khasi, Garo, Mizo, Meitei, Nepali, Nagamese, Bodo, Kokborok, Hindi, and English. 3. WCAG 2.1 AAA Contrast: All text meets strict 7:1 contrast ratios for visibility under direct mountain sunlight."
Q3 Why is the offline mode styled with high-visibility amber and dark slate rather than regular blue?
"In disaster psychology, visual feedback must immediately confirm operational state changes. When cellular connectivity drops, the app switches to an Emergency Survival Theme with an unmistakable amber beacon line. This signals to the citizen immediately that their device is conserving battery and transmitting over Bluetooth mesh."

🔬 ROLE 2: AI / DATA SCIENCE RESEARCHER & GEOLOGIST

LandslideNet v3.2 • GSI NLSM Standards • Feature Importance • False-Positive Mitigation

Q4 Why does 24-hour Cumulative Precipitation hold the highest weight (52%) over slope angle?
"Geotechnically, steep slopes are a passive conditioning factor, whereas precipitation is the active dynamic trigger. A 45° slope of Disang shale can stand stable throughout dry winter months. However, when antecedent rainfall exceeds the 140 mm / 24h threshold, pore-water pressure increases exponentially, reducing the effective normal stress ($\sigma' = \sigma - u$) to zero and triggering instantaneous translational shear failure."
Researcher Tip: Mentioning the Terzaghi effective stress equation ($\sigma' = \sigma - u$) will instantly establish domain authority with geotechnical judges.
Q5 How does your system prevent false alarms that cause public panic and evacuation fatigue?
"We utilize a Dual-Threshold Confirmation Gating mechanism. High rainfall alone cannot trigger a Red Critical Alert unless the secondary soil saturation sensor crosses 80% or the telemetry strain sensor registers displacement rate $>2.0$ mm/h. If only rainfall is high but the slope remains dry, the system caps the status at Amber (Elevated Watch), preventing false evacuations."
Q6 How is your AI model running inside a client app that is only 5 MB without a Python runtime?
"We performed Feature Weight Linearization & Polynomial Pruning. Rather than bundling PyTorch or TensorFlow runtimes (which add 150+ MB), our server trains the model and extracts the converged weight coefficients ($w_1, w_2, w_3, w_4$) and normalization vectors. Evaluating this polynomial in JavaScript executes in 1.4 milliseconds and requires less than 3 KB of storage."

⚙️ ROLE 3: BACKEND & CLOUD SYSTEMS ENGINEER

FastAPI Architecture • Emergency Pub/Sub • Scalability • Offline Database Independence

Q7 Why did you choose FastAPI over traditional Django or Node.js/Express?
"FastAPI is built on Starlette and Pydantic with asynchronous ASGI event loops. In a disaster surge where thousands of devices poll for emergency updates simultaneously, FastAPI handles 15,000+ requests per second with microsecond latency. Automatic OpenAPI documentation and native asynchronous HTTP clients (`httpx`) allow concurrent background querying of Open-Meteo and GSI telemetry nodes without thread blocking."
Q8 Why did you implement citizen onboarding in local storage instead of requiring a PostgreSQL database?
"In emergency systems, architectural resilience requires zero-dependency design. If citizen registration required an online database, a villager downloading the app in a poor 2G network would get stuck on a loading spinner and never access life-saving shelter maps. By storing Citizen Node ID, preferred language, and offline outbox in `localStorage`, the app works 100% reliably with zero server cost, zero database latency, and zero risk of database downtime."
Backend Tip: State that central database synchronization is treated as an eventual consistency enhancement, never a single point of failure.
Q9 How does your Emergency Dispatch endpoint broadcast across 5 channels simultaneously?
"Our `/api/alerts/broadcast` router utilizes an Asynchronous Fan-Out Dispatcher. When the control room triggers a broadcast order, FastAPI executes non-blocking concurrent tasks via `asyncio.gather()`: dispatching push notifications via ntfy.sh, queuing SMS gateway batches, formatting SDRF dispatch telexes, and updating the public active alert cache."

💻 ROLE 4: FRONTEND & MOBILE SOFTWARE ENGINEER

React • Capacitor Native Wrapper • Web Bluetooth API • OTA Live Updates • PWA

Q10 How do Live Over-The-Air (OTA) updates work without forcing users to re-download APKs?
"We configured Capacitor's `server.url` to point dynamically to our continuous deployment endpoint (`https://lloydaegis.netlify.app`). When we push code to GitHub `main`, Netlify automatically builds the bundle in 30 seconds. The next time the user launches the app, Capacitor streams the new JavaScript bundle into native memory. If the user is offline, Capacitor falls back instantly to the local compiled assets in `dist/`."
Q11 How does the Web Bluetooth API work inside a mobile browser without internet?
"The Web Bluetooth API (`navigator.bluetooth`) interfaces directly with the Android device's hardware Bluetooth controller via the Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Generic Attribute Profile (GATT). Even with SIM cards removed and Airplane Mode enabled, the 2.4 GHz BLE radio remains operational. AEGIS broadcasts and listens on a dedicated 16-bit Disaster Service UUID (`0xFEAA`), exchanging compact 128-byte hazard payloads without requiring OS-level PIN pairing."
Frontend Tip: Emphasize that BLE uses less than 15 mW of power, preserving smartphone battery for over 48 hours during an active power cut.
Q12 How does the GPS Safety Check work when mobile data and cellular towers are completely dead?
"Smartphones have dedicated hardware GNSS/GPS receiver chips that communicate directly with orbital satellite constellations (GPS, GLONASS, NavIC) at 1.5 GHz. Cell towers and Wi-Fi are only used for Assisted-GPS (A-GPS) speedup. In total blackout, the device still acquires raw latitude and longitude. AEGIS runs the Haversine Great-Circle Distance algorithm entirely client-side against the embedded offline fault line database to output immediate safety status."
